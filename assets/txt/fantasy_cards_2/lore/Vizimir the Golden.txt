Vizimir the Golden believes that anything worth doing is worth doing extravagantly. A master of transmutation, he earned his title after developing a spell capable of turning nearly any mundane substance into gleaming gold—a discovery that made him fabulously wealthy and briefly collapsed the economy of three neighboring kingdoms. Vizimir dresses accordingly, favoring gilded robes, jeweled rings, and a magnificent golden staff that requires two servants to polish properly. Beneath all the spectacle, however, lies a genuinely brilliant wizard. Vizimir considers magic the highest form of art, and every spell he casts is intended to be not merely effective, but beautiful.

Yet ordinary gold no longer interests Vizimir. Having already acquired more wealth than he could spend in several lifetimes, he now seeks to create something that has eluded every alchemist before him: **perfect gold**, a mythical substance said to be utterly indestructible and capable of holding limitless magical power. Vizimir travels from kingdom to kingdom collecting rare metals, forgotten alchemical texts, and increasingly dangerous magical ingredients for his experiments. He insists that perfect gold will be his masterpiece and his gift to the world. His critics suspect he mostly wants the satisfaction of engraving **VIZIMIR MADE THIS** across something that will survive until the end of time.
