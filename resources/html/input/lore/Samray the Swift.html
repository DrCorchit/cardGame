Samray the Swift

Samray was once an unremarkable court wizard with an extraordinary talent for being in the right place at precisely the wrong time. During a routine attempt to predict the following morning’s weather, he accidentally peered several thousand years into the future—and saw something that terrified him. Samray immediately abandoned his comfortable post, burned his notes, and began traveling the world in search of seemingly insignificant people, objects, and places. He has prevented assassinations, stolen spoons, rescued condemned criminals, planted trees in the middle of battlefields, and once spent nine years guarding a completely ordinary-looking chicken. When questioned, he simply replies, “It will make sense later.”

Today, Samray is obsessed with preventing the future he witnessed, though he refuses to tell anyone exactly what that future contains. His magic revolves around manipulating chance, arranging coincidences, and nudging events toward outcomes only he seems to understand. Unfortunately, decades of staring into possible futures have made it difficult for Samray to distinguish between things that *will* happen, things that *might* happen, and things that happened in timelines that no longer exist. His schemes therefore appear equal parts brilliant and completely insane—but Samray remains convinced that every bizarre action is another move in a game whose final turn may still be centuries away.
