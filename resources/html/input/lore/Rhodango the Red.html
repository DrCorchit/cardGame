Rhodango the Red

Rhodango the Red abandoned civilization long ago, declaring cities “an elaborate conspiracy against trees” before disappearing into the deepest reaches of the wilderness. There he built a crooked little cottage beneath the roots of an enormous oak and devoted himself to studying the old magic of growing things. Birds nest in his tangled red hair, foxes sleep beside his hearth, and the surrounding forest has grown so accustomed to his presence that branches bend aside when he walks past. Rhodango rarely welcomes visitors, though anyone arriving with seeds, interesting mushrooms, or good tea stands a reasonable chance of being invited inside.

Despite his reclusive nature, Rhodango is a formidable wizard and fiercely protective of the wild places he calls home. When loggers, monsters, or overly ambitious kings threaten the wilderness, the mild-mannered hermit becomes considerably less mild. He can awaken ancient trees, command tangled roots to swallow armies, and turn barren earth green with a sweep of his staff. Rhodango insists he has no interest in the affairs of civilization, yet he regularly emerges from seclusion whenever nature is threatened. Asked why he keeps helping people he claims not to like, Rhodango usually grumbles something about “protecting the forest from whatever nonsense you lot have started this time.”
