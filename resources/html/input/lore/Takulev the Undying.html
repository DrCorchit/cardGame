Takulev the Undying

Takulev the Undying’s earliest memory is drowning to death in the Swamps of Mordun. His second is waking several hours later beneath the lantern of a passing monk who, through either compassion or spectacularly poor judgment, performed a forbidden rite to restore him to life. The ritual succeeded only in the loosest sense. Takulev rose again, but whatever part of him once felt pain, fear, affection, or remorse remained behind in the swamp. He has existed ever since in a state of permanent undeath, his body stubbornly refusing to die and his lifeforce sustained by pure ambition. Over the centuries, Takulev has become a master of necromancy, assembling a vast knowledge of forbidden rituals through which he summons, constructs, and occasionally improves upon magical abominations that sensible wizards would prefer remained theoretical.

What Takulev actually wants is considerably harder to determine. He has accumulated forbidden grimoires, robbed tombs whose occupants were still using them, and ventured repeatedly into the Abyss in pursuit of its legendary wealth. Gold alone seems unlikely to motivate a creature with no appetite for luxury, yet Takulev has never explained what he intends to do with the riches he seeks. Some believe he is preparing a ritual capable of restoring what he lost in the Swamps of Mordun; others suspect he is assembling the components for something far worse. Takulev neither confirms nor denies these theories. Whatever his purpose, an undying necromancer with limitless patience, no fear of death, and an army of abominations probably has motives that are better left unknown.
