Welcome to Wizard Wars!

A game of strategy, sorcery, and spectacularly questionable decisions.

In this world, five great factions vie for power, each drawing upon its own people, resources, and peculiar traditions. Ancient forests, sprawling cities, storm-tossed seas, shadowy criminal empires, and fire-forged kingdoms all collide in a struggle where clever planning can be just as important as raw strength.

In Wizard Wars, every faction approaches the conflict differently. Avalon harnesses the power of nature, rewarding growth and diversity while building toward devastating finishers. Metropolis relies on steampunk elves and ingenious machines, using utility and countermeasures to keep its opponents from executing their plans. Thalassa takes the direct approach, sending Ships, Pirates, and Warriors into battle with little interest in subtlety. Transylvania prefers to fight on its opponent's terms—or prevent them from fighting at all—using vampires, criminals, and slayers to dismantle strategies before they can develop. Vulcania relies on dwarven industry, sturdy defenses, drunken revelry, and mighty dragons, enduring attacks while making the most of its comparatively limited offensive power.

Your choice of faction will shape the way you play, but no faction is limited to a single strategy. Learning when to press an advantage, when to conserve your resources, and when to disrupt your opponent's plans is essential to mastering Wizard Wars. As you become familiar with the cards at your disposal, you'll discover combinations, tactics, and unexpected interactions that allow you to put your own spin on your faction's strengths. Choose your allegiance, prepare your deck, and enter the fray—because in Wizard Wars, there is always another spell to cast, another scheme to hatch, and another battle waiting to begin.
