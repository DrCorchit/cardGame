A Trading Card Game of strategy, sorcery, and intelligent decisions.

Wizard Wars is a fantasy trading card game that brings people together to experience complex strategy, powerful combos, and rich lore with friends both new and old. The game's innovative 3-round structure welcomes flexible strategies and backup plans, while the provision system ensures that no single card receives all the glory. Whether you love building clever decks, exploring strange fantasy worlds, discovering powerful card combinations, or simply outwitting the player across the table, Wizard Wars offers something both new and fun to everyone.
